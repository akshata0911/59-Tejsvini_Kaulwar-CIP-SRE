    How to read this dashboard

    Think of a shop with billing counters. Customers are the traffic and counters are the servers. The load balancer shares customers between counters. The auto-scaler opens a new counter when it gets crowded and closes one when it is quiet. Self-healing replaces a counter that breaks. Press Run demo to watch the whole story.
